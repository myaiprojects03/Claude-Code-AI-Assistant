# Frequently asked questions

## How much do your services cost?
We don't quote prices in chat. Every engagement is scoped to your needs, so pricing is discussed with a consultant after a discovery call, and confirmed in a written proposal.

## What is a discovery call?
A free, 30-minute video call with one of our consultants. We ask about your goals, current systems and constraints, and explain how we could help. There's no obligation, and you'll receive a short summary afterwards.

## Who do you work with?
Organisations of all sizes, from businesses with around ten staff to enterprises with thousands of employees. We work across most sectors, including professional services, manufacturing, retail, education and the non-profit sector.

## Where are you based and do you work remotely?
Our team is based in the United Kingdom. Most work is delivered remotely, and consultants can visit your site when a project needs it.

## What are your office hours?
Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:30 UK time, excluding public holidays. Existing managed-service clients have their own support arrangements, described in their service agreement.

## How quickly can you start?
It depends on the scope and our team's availability. A consultant will discuss timing with you on the discovery call. We don't commit to start dates in chat.

## Do you only work with Microsoft?
No. We work with Microsoft, Google and the major cloud providers (Azure, AWS and Google Cloud), and we recommend whatever fits your needs best.

## Can you help with a security incident right now?
If you think you're experiencing a security incident (for example ransomware, a suspected breach or a compromised account), tell the assistant straight away and it will alert our team. For ongoing incident response, we'll connect you with a consultant.

## Do you sign NDAs?
Yes. We're happy to sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement before discussing confidential details.

## Can I become a client if I'm outside the UK?
Yes. We work with organisations in the UK and Europe, and occasionally further afield. Mention your location on the discovery call.
