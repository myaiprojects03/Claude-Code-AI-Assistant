# Template: Meeting summary

## Format
Subject: [Client] - [Meeting] - [Date]

- Attendees
- Purpose (one line)
- Decisions (numbered; each one stated as a decision, not a discussion)
- Actions (owner, action, due date)
- Risks or open questions
- Next meeting

Send within one working day. Keep it to one screen.
