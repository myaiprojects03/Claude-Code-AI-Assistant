# Template: Client proposal

## Structure
1. Summary: the client's goal in their words, and the outcome we will deliver (one paragraph).
2. Current situation: what we learned in discovery.
3. Approach: phases, each with objectives and deliverables.
4. Plan: indicative phase durations. Dates are only confirmed after kickoff.
5. Team: named lead consultant and roles.
6. Commercials: fixed price per phase or day-rate estimate, payment milestones, assumptions.
7. Risks and dependencies.
8. Next steps.

## Style
Plain English, the client's terminology, no unexplained acronyms. Never reuse another client's proposal text; start from this template.
