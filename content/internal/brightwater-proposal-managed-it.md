# Brightwater Housing: managed IT proposal

## Summary
Fully managed IT for Brightwater Housing's 310 staff across four offices, including the service desk, device management and the tenant repairs portal used by 1,140 tenants.

## Approach
Onboard in six weeks: discovery, tooling (Intune, Defender for Business), knowledge transfer from the outgoing provider, then go-live with a 30-day hypercare period.
