# Template: Follow-up email after a discovery call

## Structure
1. Thank them for their time (one sentence).
2. Summarise what we heard: goals and constraints (three bullets maximum).
3. Proposed next step and when they'll hear from us.
4. Sign off with the lead consultant's name and direct contact.

## Rules
No prices or dates unless a proposal has already been agreed internally. Warm, concise, British English.
