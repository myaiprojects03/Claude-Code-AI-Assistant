# SOP: Security incident response

## First hour
1. The on-call security consultant owns the incident until handed over in writing.
2. Contain first: isolate affected devices, disable compromised accounts, revoke active sessions and app passwords.
3. Preserve evidence: export sign-in and audit logs before changing anything else; take memory captures where possible.
4. Open a P1 ticket and a dedicated incident channel; log every action with a timestamp.

## Within 24 hours
- Agree with the client whether the ICO must be notified (personal data breach: within 72 hours of awareness).
- Reset credentials in priority order: admins, finance, executives, then everyone else.
- Brief the client's leadership twice a day until contained.

## Afterwards
Write a post-incident report within ten working days: timeline, root cause, what worked, and remediation with owners.
