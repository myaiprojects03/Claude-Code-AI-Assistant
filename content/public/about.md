# About us and how we work

## Who we are
Acme IT Consulting is an independent IT consultancy of around 50 people: consultants, engineers, project managers and a service desk team.

## How an engagement works
1. Discovery call: we learn about your goals and current situation.
2. Proposal: we send a written proposal with scope, approach and commercials.
3. Delivery: a named consultant or project manager leads the work, with regular updates.
4. Handover and support: documentation, training and, if you wish, ongoing managed support.

## Privacy and your data
Conversations with this assistant are stored so our team can follow up, and are handled under UK GDPR. We only use your details to respond to your enquiry. You can ask us to export or delete your data at any time by contacting our team. See our privacy notice for full details.

## Talking to a person
You can ask to speak to a consultant at any point in the conversation. During office hours, a consultant will follow up as soon as possible. Outside office hours, we'll reply on the next working day.
