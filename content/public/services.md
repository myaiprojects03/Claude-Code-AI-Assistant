# Our services

Acme IT Consulting helps small, mid-sized and enterprise organisations plan, run and secure their IT. Every engagement starts with a free discovery call so we understand your situation before recommending anything.

## Cloud migration
We plan and carry out moves from on-premises servers to Microsoft Azure, AWS or Google Cloud, or between clouds. Work typically includes an assessment of your current systems, a migration plan, a pilot, the migration itself, and handover with documentation. We also help with hybrid set-ups where some systems stay on-premises.

## Managed IT and support
An ongoing service where our team looks after your users, devices, servers and cloud services. It includes a service desk for your staff, monitoring, patching, backup checks and regular reviews. It suits organisations without an in-house IT team, or teams that want extra capacity.

## Cybersecurity assessments
Independent reviews of your security posture: vulnerability assessments, configuration reviews of Microsoft 365 and cloud tenants, policy reviews, and a prioritised remediation plan. We can also help you prepare for frameworks such as Cyber Essentials or ISO 27001. We do not offer a 24/7 security operations centre.

## Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace
Set-up, migration and optimisation of Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace: email and calendar migrations, Teams and SharePoint or Drive structure, identity and device management, licensing reviews and staff training.

## Data and analytics
We help you bring data together and make it usable: data warehousing, reporting with Power BI or Looker Studio, data quality work, and dashboards for management teams.

## Custom software and integrations
Bespoke integrations between business systems (for example CRM, finance and ticketing tools), workflow automation, internal web applications and APIs.

## IT strategy and vCIO
A part-time, senior IT leader for organisations that don't need a full-time CIO: IT roadmaps, budgeting support, vendor selection, risk management and board-level reporting.
