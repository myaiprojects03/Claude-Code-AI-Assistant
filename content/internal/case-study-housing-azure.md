# Case study (anonymised): Housing association moves to Azure

## Situation
A 400-person housing association ran its housing management system on ageing on-premises servers with no disaster recovery.

## What we did
Assessment, Azure landing zone, migration of 23 servers in four waves, and backup and DR with a tested recovery plan.

## Results
Recovery time objective fell from several days to four hours; server count fell by a third. This anonymised version may be shared with prospects.
